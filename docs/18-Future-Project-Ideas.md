# Future Projects After RideX

Once RideX has a stable platform foundation, the strongest follow-on projects are those that reuse its infrastructure.

## 1. RideX FleetOS
Fleet management SaaS for vehicle owners:
- vehicles
- maintenance
- driver assignments
- fuel
- earnings
- compliance

## 2. RideX Delivery
Last-mile delivery platform using the same dispatch/location infrastructure.

## 3. RideX Corporate
Corporate mobility:
- employee rides
- billing
- ride policies
- corporate wallets
- reporting

## 4. RideX Logistics
B2B logistics orchestration:
- shipment creation
- driver assignment
- route optimization
- proof of delivery

## 5. RideX Pay
Mobility-focused payment/ledger infrastructure:
- wallets
- refunds
- driver payouts
- settlements
- reconciliation

## 6. RideX Safety
Standalone mobility safety platform:
- emergency workflows
- incident management
- trip monitoring
- risk scoring
- audit

## 7. RideX Dispatch Engine
A reusable dispatch engine that can serve:
- ride-hailing
- delivery
- service technicians
- logistics

## 8. RideX Analytics
Mobility analytics:
- demand heatmaps
- driver utilization
- ETA accuracy
- cancellation patterns
- revenue analytics

## Recommended sequence

RideX B2C → FleetOS → RideX Delivery → Corporate Mobility → Dispatch Engine/Analytics.
